    Roff – The Tile & Stone Fixing Expert

    Roff, from the house of Pidilite Industries Ltd., India’s leading adhesives company, provides cutting-edge tile and stone fixing solutions. It has developed a vast portfolio of technologically advanced products with global expertise merged with local intelligence; making them tile and stone fixing experts. With a complete range right from waterproof floor tile adhesive to wall tiles fixing chemical, Roff has it all. In fact, numerous years in the industry has made Roff intuitive about the current market needs and demands, from the perspective of both architects as well as consumers.


    With over 20,000+ partnering architects and contractors, Roff has an unparalleled network in the Industry. Moreover, they have 300+ trained experts who strive to deliver the best personalized solution to your projects


    Roff Tile and stone fixing solutions add both beauty and durability to your home. In comparison to the average tile adhesive price in India, Roff tile and stone adhesives come at an affordable cost making the roff adhesive price very competitive.


    Roff’s best-in-class global collaborations with other industry experts have played a pivotal role in creating niche-specific advanced products. Pidilite has partnered with Tenax, Italy to provide stone fixing solutions that help to retain the magnificence of your homes in the long run. Joint Venture with Litokol, Italy ensures exclusive distribution of its wide range of products through Pidilite. Spanish expert in tile adhesives & technical mortar technology, Grupopama is in a joint venture with Pidilite to provide a world class tile adhesive range in the Indian market.


    Being a leading tile and stone fixing expert with a vast portfolio of technologically driven advanced products; Roff offers aesthetics and endurance to your spaces.

    Tile Joint Filler Visualizer
    In a technologically-driven new age, Roff is bringing in smart techniques to help you make the right and quick decisions. The Tile Joint Filler Visualizer is a first of its kind tool to visualize different tile joint fillers with various tile designs.


    Grout Coverage Calculator
    It’s a 2-step method that helps to estimate the right quantity of tile joint fillers as per your project’s need.


    Adhesive Coverage Calculator
    It’s a 2-step method to arrive at the exact quantity of tile adhesive, considering that each tile adhesive coverage is different depending on its properties.


    Tile Adhesive Selector
    The tile adhesive selector is a comprehensive tool to help you choose the right tile adhesive online based on your selection of room, type & size of tile, and type of surface.
